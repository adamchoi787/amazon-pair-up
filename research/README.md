## How to read the numbers

- Market and pain-point figures come from public reports, linked where they are used.
- Figures marked **(model)** are our own estimates; their inputs are written next to
  them so anyone can change an assumption and recompute.
- Pilot results will be added to IMPACT.md as they come in.
