# PairUp in plain words

*A five-minute read. The numbers and their sources are in [IMPACT.md](IMPACT.md);
the research papers are in [LITERATURE.md](LITERATURE.md).*

## A small problem that happens all the time

Picture this: it's the night before a math exam at the University of Washington. Maya's
calculator just died. Two buildings away, Sam has an old TI-84 in a drawer that he
hasn't touched since last year.

They will probably never find each other. Maya would have to post in a group chat
or scroll a resale site; Sam would have to take photos, write a listing, answer
"is this still available?", argue over two dollars and arrange a meet-up. For a
$20 calculator, neither bothers. Maya buys a new one for over $100. Sam's
calculator stays in the drawer.

The same thing happens with small jobs. Carrying a $5 lunch from the student union
to a dorm takes two minutes. Arranging it through a delivery app costs more than the
lunch is worth.

## What PairUp does

You just say what you need, have, or can do:

> "Alexa, sell my TI-84 for $20. I'll take anything above $15."
>
> "Alexa, I need a graphing calculator today, max $18."

PairUp finds the match, works out a fair price by a fixed rule, picks a safe
public place to meet, and asks both people for a simple yes. That's it: Maya pays
$18, Sam receives $18, and the calculator gets used again.

## Our three promises

### 1. Faster and cheaper to buy what you need

The thing you need is often a short walk away, with someone who would happily part
with it. PairUp connects you in one sentence. There's no shipping, no waiting a
week, and no platform taking a cut. Resale sites usually keep 13–20% of every sale;
on PairUp the whole price goes from one person to the other.

### 2. Make personal business great

Everyone has a little business in them: textbooks to sell at the end of the
quarter, a free afternoon, a car on Saturdays, a habit of walking past the
pharmacy. Today each one needs a separate app, a profile and a fee.

With PairUp, starting is one sentence: "I do lunch runs from the HUB on weekdays,
$4." You set your price and your hours. You receive the full price. Good ratings
bring you more customers, and PairUp remembers regular requests so your regulars
come back.

More than 72 million Americans already earn money working for themselves, and
nearly a third of adults under 30 have done gig work. PairUp gives them a simpler
front door.

### 3. C2C in the future

Soon, many people will have an AI assistant that shops and sells for them. Those
assistants need a trustworthy place to meet. PairUp is built for that:

- Any assistant can connect: Alexa, or any AI agent that speaks the open MCP
  standard.
- **Assistants never haggle.** Research shows AI negotiators can be swayed by a
  sob story or a pushy tone. PairUp sets every price with one published rule
  instead, so nobody can talk their way to a better deal.
- **A person always says the final yes.** Your private limits stay private.

## Why it matters beyond one calculator

- **Money stays with people.** If one in ten UW students used PairUp twice a month
  for $15 exchanges, about $1.9 million a year would change hands directly between
  students. That's our estimate; the inputs are listed in [IMPACT.md](IMPACT.md).
- **Less waste.** About 80% of discarded furniture in the US ends up in landfill.
  Every June, students throw away fridges, desks and lamps that next year's
  students will buy new. PairUp passes them on.
- **Safer than a stranger online.** Americans lost $12.5 billion to fraud in 2024,
  and online shopping was the second most reported category. PairUp uses
  signed-in accounts, ratings, a warning when a price looks too good to be true,
  public meet-up points, and payment only when you're face to face.
- **Useful stuff gets used.** The average US home holds $3,000–4,000 of things that
  could be resold. When selling takes one sentence, they get sold.

## Where it starts

PairUp starts on the University of Washington campus in Seattle: 52,000 students
within walking distance, with needs that come back every quarter (textbooks,
calculators, move-in, move-out). Next come the other Seattle campuses and
neighborhoods, then campuses across the US, where about 19 million students live
the same year on repeat.

## The idea in one line

**Say what you need. A neighbor has it. PairUp makes the deal fair, safe and fast.**
