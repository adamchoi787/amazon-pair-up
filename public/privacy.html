AmazonPairUp privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

AmazonPairUp pairs people who need something with people nearby who have it or can do it, for things and for small jobs such as errands or event help, by voice, through its web app and as an Alexa+ add-on. It is an independent project built for the Amazon Developer Hackathon 2026.

What we store

  • Account: the display name you enter when you sign in (in the web app or when linking Alexa), and an internal user id.
  • Listings: items you sell, including the asking price and the private minimum price you set.
  • Buy requests: what you're looking for, including the private maximum price you set, and the words of your request.
  • Deals: agreed prices, confirmation status and meet-up area.
  • Activity log: a record of marketplace actions, used to run and debug the service.

Private price limits

Your minimum selling price and maximum buying price are used only on our server to decide whether a deal is possible and at what price. They are never shown to other users.

Sign-in tokens

When you sign in, the web app (or Alexa, when you link your account) receives tokens that let it act on the marketplace for you. We store only one-way hashes of these tokens. The web app keeps its tokens in your browser's storage until you sign out.

Processing

Text of listings, requests and assistant conversations is processed by Amazon Web Services (Amazon Bedrock) to understand requests and find matches. Voice is turned into text by your browser's speech service before it reaches us. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Listings, requests and deals are kept while the service runs, and backups for seven days. Take down your own posts any time in the app ("My posts"). To have your account and data deleted, contact us through the project repository.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at the project's GitHub repository.
