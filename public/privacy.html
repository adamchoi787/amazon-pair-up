VoiceMarket privacy policy

Hackathon prototype · Last updated October 18, 2026

VoiceMarket is a prototype second-hand marketplace you use through Alexa+. It was built for the Amazon Developer Hackathon 2026 and isn't a commercial service.

What we store

  • Account: the display name you enter when you link VoiceMarket to Alexa, and an internal user id.
  • Listings: items you sell, including the asking price and the private minimum price you set.
  • Buy requests: what you're looking for, including the private maximum price you set, and the words of your request.
  • Deals: agreed prices, confirmation status and meet-up area.
  • Activity log: a record of marketplace actions, used to run and debug the service.

Private price limits

Your minimum selling price and maximum buying price are used only on our server to decide whether a deal is possible and at what price. They are never shown to other users.

Sign-in tokens

When you link your account, Alexa receives tokens that let it act on the marketplace for you. We store only one-way hashes of these tokens.

Processing

Text of listings and requests may be processed by Amazon Web Services (Amazon Bedrock) to understand requests and find matches. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

This is a prototype, and demo data may be reset at any time. To have your data deleted, unlink VoiceMarket in the Alexa app and contact us through the project repository.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at the project's GitHub repository.
